Company Dashboard

Overview​

The Company Dashboard provides a consolidated operational view of all managed components associated with a specific company. It enables support users to monitor component health, review operational activity, analyze management effectiveness, and investigate situations affecting services delivered to a customer, business unit, or organizational entity.

The dashboard aggregates information from all components belonging to the selected company and presents it through multiple operational views, helping support teams quickly assess overall service health and identify areas requiring attention.

Purpose​

The Company Dashboard helps support users to:

  • Assess the overall operational health of a company.
  • Monitor the status of all associated managed components.
  • Review operational situations impacting the company.
  • Analyze technology-specific service health.
  • Evaluate monitoring effectiveness.
  • Investigate incidents across multiple components.
  • Access inventory and management information.
  • Generate customer-oriented operational views and reports.

Accessing Company Information​

The dashboard can be accessed by searching for the desired company through the ITOSS search interface. Once selected, all operational and management information associated with that company becomes available through the dashboard.

Company Summary​

The upper section of the dashboard provides a high-level summary of the company and the operational condition of its managed environment.

Available Information​

The overview typically includes:

  • Company identification information.
  • Managed component inventory.
  • Operational status distribution.
  • Availability summary.
  • Consolidated health indicators.

This information allows support users to quickly understand the current condition of the company’s managed infrastructure and services.

Information Cards​

The dashboard includes a set of operational cards that summarize activity across all components associated with the company.

These cards provide both statistical information and direct access to detailed operational records.

Dashboard Entries Card​

Provides visibility into operational situations that have been attended by support teams.

Typical Uses​

  • Review recent operational activity.
  • Analyze incident history.
  • Evaluate operational workload.

User Notes Card​

Provides access to operational notes documented for any component belonging to the company.

Typical Uses​

  • Review investigation findings.
  • Access operational recommendations.
  • Share knowledge among support teams.

Operations Log Card​

Displays operational records generated by monitoring and management activities.

Typical Uses​

  • Troubleshooting.
  • Audit reviews.
  • Diagnostic analysis.

Tickets Card​

Provides visibility into service desk tickets associated with the company’s components.

Typical Uses​

  • Incident tracking.
  • Correlation between operational situations and support processes.
  • Service management reviews.

Lifecycle Changes Card​

Displays lifecycle-related activities affecting company components.

Typical Uses​

  • Maintenance tracking.
  • Operational change review.
  • Lifecycle status validation.

Scheduled Jobs Card​

Displays activity associated with scheduled operational tasks.

Typical Uses​

  • Verify task execution.
  • Review scheduled process activity.
  • Monitor automation results.

Technology and Management Overview​

The dashboard organizes components according to technology and management scope. Components are displayed within expandable groups that allow support users to progressively navigate operational information.

Available Information​

For each component, the dashboard may display:

  • Operational status.
  • Management information.
  • Configuration attributes.
  • Ownership information.
  • Management scope.

Operational Benefits​

This organization allows operators to:

  • Analyze specific technology domains.
  • Review related components together.
  • Isolate issues affecting particular technologies.
  • Understand management responsibilities.

Component Search​

A component search capability is available within the dashboard. Search operations evaluate all visible attributes associated with the displayed components.

Search Scope​

Searches may match:

  • Operational attributes.
  • Administrative information.
  • Inventory data.
  • Configuration parameters.
  • Management information.

Typical Use Cases​

  • Locate a specific server or application.
  • Search by configuration value.
  • Find components associated with a specific operational attribute.
  • Identify related infrastructure elements.

Operational Indicators

The Company Dashboard includes the same key operational indicators available in Operational Dashboards, but aggregated for all components belonging to the selected company.

Component Status​

Provides a consolidated view of operational availability across all managed components.

Status Categories​

StatusDescription
UpComponent is operational
DownComponent is unavailable or experiencing operational issues

Dashboard Entries by Priority​

Displays the distribution of operational situations according to priority.

Operational Value​

  • Rapid identification of critical situations.
  • Prioritization of support activities.
  • Workload visibility.

Management Quality​

Provides an aggregate view of monitoring effectiveness and operational management quality for the company’s components.

Operational Value​

  • Identify monitoring coverage issues.
  • Validate management effectiveness.
  • Detect gaps in operational visibility.

Dashboard Views

In addition to the primary summary view, the Company Dashboard provides several specialized operational views.

Dashboard Entries View​

The Dashboard Entries view displays all operational situations generated by the company’s components.

Capabilities​

  • View active situations.
  • Apply operational filters.
  • Analyze dashboard entries.
  • Execute situation management actions.

Typical Use Cases​

  • Incident investigation.
  • Situation monitoring.
  • Operational response management.

Health Overview View​

This view provides a consolidated representation of component health organized by technology and management scope.

The checklist-style presentation enables support users to rapidly identify unhealthy components and technology domains requiring investigation.

Typical Use Cases​

  • Daily operational reviews.
  • Customer health assessments.
  • Technology-specific analysis.

Management Quality View​

This view focuses on monitoring effectiveness across all company components.

Support users can evaluate whether components are being managed correctly and determine if issues exist within the monitoring infrastructure itself.

Typical Use Cases​

  • Monitoring validation.
  • Coverage reviews.
  • Operational quality assessments.

Components View​

The Components View provides a complete inventory of all company components, regardless of lifecycle state.

Included Lifecycle States​

  • Delivery
  • Operations
  • Maintenance
  • Out of Service
  • End of Service

Capabilities​

  • Inventory review.
  • Component search.
  • Advanced filtering.
  • Operational analysis.

Typical Use Cases​

  • Asset discovery.
  • Inventory validation.
  • Component lifecycle analysis.

Custom Dashboards

The Company Dashboard supports the creation of custom operational dashboards based on selected components and management containers.

Purpose​

Custom dashboards allow organizations to create operational views aligned with business services, technology services, or other logical operational groupings.

Typical Examples​

  • Business applications.
  • Customer-facing services.
  • Infrastructure services.
  • Technology platforms.

Benefits​

  • Service-oriented monitoring.
  • Business-focused operational visibility.
  • Simplified operational governance.

Reporting

The Company Dashboard provides access to reporting capabilities based on historical component information.

Report Generation​

Reports can be generated:

  • On demand.
  • Through scheduled execution processes.

Custom Reports​

Organizations may implement custom HTML templates to create reports tailored to business, operational, or executive requirements.

Typical Use Cases​

  • Customer reporting.
  • Service reviews.
  • Executive summaries.
  • Operational performance reporting.

Operational Considerations

  • The Company Dashboard provides a customer-centric view of the managed environment.
  • All operational indicators represent aggregated information across the company's components.
  • Technology-based organization helps isolate issues affecting specific service areas.
  • Multiple views support operational analysis from health, monitoring, incident, and inventory perspectives.
  • Custom dashboards enable business service monitoring beyond individual components.
  • Historical reporting provides visibility into operational performance and service quality.
